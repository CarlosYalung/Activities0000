About Pickleball

Pickleball is a fun paddle sport created for all skill levels that combines elements of tennis, badminton, and table tennis. Played on a small court with a perforated plastic ball, it is easy to learn yet fast-paced and competitive.

At AAA Courts, we provide state-of-the-art facilities, premium outdoor/indoor surfacing, and a welcoming community for casual and pro players alike.
